# PRD, UX DNA & Requirement Specifications: NexusTrade AI

## UX DNA & Information Architecture
- **Navigation Bar**: Brand identity, Stage Stepper (US-001 -> US-005), Ticker Quick Selector, Latency Indicator, Export Modal CTA.
- **Workspace Canvas**: Split-screen Left Analysis Configurator & Right Live Dual-Output Viewer with Interactive Candlestick Chart.
- **Confluence Scoring Engine**: Real-time Gauge Meter, 10-Indicator Bento Grid, Weighted bias attribution.
- **Risk Calculator**: Interactive Capital, Risk %, Stop-Loss, and Target Take-Profit positioning with dynamic RRR.
- **Context Chain Inspector**: Deterministic DAG dependency visualization across stages.
- **Export & Privacy Modal**: JSON/CSV artifact generator with GDPR data export & erasure hooks.

## User Stories & Acceptance Criteria
- **[P0] US-001**: Smart Search & Multi-Timeframe Ticker (15m, 1H, 4H, 1D).
- **[P0] US-002**: Modular 10-Indicator Engine (Price Action, VWAP, RSI, Ichimoku, Fibonacci, MACD, SMC, ICT, Elliott Wave, TD Sequential).
- **[P0] US-003**: AI Confluence Aggregator (Weighted 0-100 score, Bullish/Bearish/Neutral bias, Gemini narrative report).
- **[P1] US-004**: Risk & Position Sizing Calculator (Entry, SL, TP1-3, RRR, lot size, leverage).
- **[P1] US-005**: Interactive Modular UI (Collapsible bento grid, dual-output tabs, context DAG graph, export suite).
