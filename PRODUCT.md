# PRODUCT.md - Product Context & Voice Specification
**Project:** NexusTrade AI: Multi-Indicator Institutional Crypto Analyzer  
**Architecture Standard:** Impeccable Style (impeccable.style)

## 1. Product Vision & Mission
Build a high-craft institutional cryptocurrency confluence analyzer and risk planner that eliminates analysis paralysis by synthesizing 10 modular indicator engines and providing mathematically grounded confidence scores in < 30 seconds.

## 2. Target Personas
- **Scalper:** Requires sub-second multi-frame confluence signals and instant key levels.
- **Institutional Analyst:** Demands objective mathematical validation free of subjective chart illusions.
- **Data-Driven Trader:** Executes structured rules with precise risk-reward sizing and capital preservation.
- **Quantitative Risk Manager:** Mandates dynamic stop-loss, take-profit ladders, and liquidation buffers.

## 3. Brand Voice & Tone
- Direct & Authoritative: Concise, technical, and jargon-free.
- Craftsmanship First: Every label, button, metric, and error boundary is refined and purposeful.
- Anti-Slop Directives: Zero generic marketing hype, no fake placeholder stats, strict functional clarity.
